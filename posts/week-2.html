Building in public

Published on Oct 20, 2026

Building in public means letting people see the messy middle and not only the polished finish.

For a long time, I thought visibility meant waiting until everything was finished. That only delayed progress. Showing the process creates accountability, invites feedback, and often leads to better decisions.

What this looks like

  • Share small wins and honest setbacks.
  • Document what is working and what is not.
  • Let the work evolve in front of people.

It is not about sharing everything perfectly. It is about being clear, useful, and genuine.
