About me

Curious about AI. Always building.

Hey, I'm Kumar, a final-year engineering student and AWS Student Builder Center Campus Lead. I love figuring out how AI models actually work and building things with them.

Lately, I've been experimenting with language models: training them, making them more efficient, and working toward building one that understands Kannada better. I'm a former ML research intern at IISc Bengaluru, and I've released open-source AI models.

I'm still learning, experimenting, and figuring things out as I go. This blog is where I share what I build, what I learn, what goes wrong, and the interesting things I discover along the way.
